Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a government scheme?

Go to the Schemes section and browse schemes by category. You can also use the search option to find schemes based on your requirements.

How can I check my eligibility?

Open a scheme to view its eligibility criteria, including age, occupation, income and other requirements.

Where can I apply for a scheme?

Scheme Finder provides the official application portal or official website wherever available. Always verify the information before submitting an application.

Can I save schemes for later?

Yes. You can save schemes that interest you and access them later from the Saved Schemes section of your dashboard.

How do I receive scheme notifications?

Notifications can keep you informed about new schemes, application deadlines, reminders and important scheme updates.
