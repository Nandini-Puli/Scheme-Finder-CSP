About Scheme Finder

Scheme Finder is a unified platform designed to make government schemes easier to discover, understand and access. Our goal is to help citizens find opportunities that match their needs, eligibility and goals — all in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify access to government schemes by bringing relevant information together in a clear and user-friendly platform. Scheme Finder helps users understand eligibility, benefits, required documents and application information without having to search across multiple websites.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every citizen can easily discover government opportunities that support their education, employment, entrepreneurship and career development. We aim to make scheme discovery simple, accessible and convenient.

Who We Help

Scheme Finder is designed for students, employed individuals, unemployed citizens, entrepreneurs and aspiring professionals looking for suitable government support, financial assistance, training opportunities and internships.

Why Scheme Finder?

Government scheme information can often be spread across different portals and departments. Scheme Finder provides a single, simple interface where users can explore schemes, check eligibility, understand benefits and access official application portals.
